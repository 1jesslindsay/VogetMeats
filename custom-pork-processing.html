Pork Processing

Quality You Can Taste, Service You Can Trust

The standard fresh cuts from whole and half hogs include arm roasts, shoulder steaks, chops, and spareribs, while the bellies are made into bacon and the hind legs into ham. However, all portions of a hog can be cured, or all portions can be kept fresh. Our standard thickness for steaks and chops is ¾”, and our standard roast size ranges between 3–4 lbs. All options include our old-fashioned drugstore wrap with waxed freezer paper, and all options are based on average hog size and quality. Hogs that are excessively lean, fat, small, or large may limit cutting/curing options. Give us a call to discuss how you would like your hog processed!
